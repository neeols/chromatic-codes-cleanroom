initializeTextEffects

@Synchronized
fun initializeTextEffects()

Publishes all 28 built-in factory IDs once, before dependent mods register formatted content.

The map preserves the actual constructor choices and defaults for all 27 concrete effect classes. Both wave and none use DynamicEffect with different branch selectors. Factories create fresh objects; MovementRegistry then validates/configures their palettes and explicit options.

The synchronized guard prevents two construction callers from republishing the factory batch. It does not prevent another mod from deliberately replacing a factory later.
